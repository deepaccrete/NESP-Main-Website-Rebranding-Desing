One initiative, one platform, one set of evidence.

How a global goal became Nigeria's national electrification map.

The initiative

Sustainable Energy for All is the global push for universal energy access. Nigeria's Action Agenda sets the 30:30:30 goal: 30,000 MW of generation by 2030 with 30% from renewables.

The platform

Nigeria SE4ALL is the national electrification data platform — settlements, buildings, grid infrastructure and mini-grids, ground-truthed by field teams and published openly.

Who it serves

Federal and state planners, mini-grid and solar developers, investors, researchers and the public — everyone planning from the same map.

All the data, open and ready to download

Everything on the webmaps is published on the Nigeria SE4ALL geodata portal: mapped settlements and buildings, field-verified MV and HV grid lines, distribution company coverage, existing and planned mini-grids, and power plants across all 36 states and the FCT.

Each dataset carries its update date, source and a Creative Commons licence, so planners, developers and researchers can use it in QGIS, Google Earth or their own models.

Learning Platform

Learn to use the platform

The Nigeria SE4ALL Learning Platform is a free online learning system that turns the data platform into skills. Structured video courses and step-by-step guides take you from reading the webmaps to downloading open datasets and running your own analysis in QGIS — learn at your own pace, track your progress, and earn a certificate when you finish.
