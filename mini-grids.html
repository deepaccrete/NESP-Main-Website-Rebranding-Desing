Mini-grids

Open data and tools for Nigeria’s mini-grid sector

We support the continual growth of the mini-grid sector through open data and tools. We have mapped over 60,000 km of electricity grid and over 3.2 million off-grid buildings, and built tools to assess this data. The data is enriched further through site visits and in-depth community mapping.
