Solar home systems

Planning data for solar home system distribution

Solar home systems have an important place in Nigeria’s off-grid energy mix. They provide a sustainable back-up power option for those living under the grid, and a sole source of clean power for those in rural areas. We work with the private sector and other government stakeholders to provide analytics that support the development of this sector.
