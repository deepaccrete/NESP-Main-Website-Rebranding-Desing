Data notes

  • Figures are calculated from the public GeoNode layers (planned refresh: nightly). Last pull: 20 November 2026.
  • Line lengths are measured after clipping to the state boundary. The MV layer combines SE4ALL field data, DisCo grid data (KAECO, KEDCO) and 2025 field work orders.
  • "Distance to the grid" is the distance recorded for each settlement cluster in the SE4ALL settlement layer. It may not yet reflect MV lines added in later field work, so it can overstate distances where new grid data has been collected.
  • Field-survey records are shown as published. Survey dates for Benue: 2022-08-15 to 2022-08-30.
  • Dataset licences are not yet specified on the data portal.
