How the data is made

From satellite to field survey

The platform combines remote mapping, field visits and vehicle-mounted mapping, then publishes the result as open layers.

75,000+

Remote mapping

Buildings mapped in off-grid areas of Kogi, Yobe and Imo through SE4ALL mapathons on OpenStreetMap.

Field survey

Settlements visited and published with grid status, income sources and services. Most were surveyed in 2022; new surveys are still being added (latest: 14 Nov 2026).
