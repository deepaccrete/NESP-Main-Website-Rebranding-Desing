About the survey

  • Source: se4allWS:cluster_offgrid_survey (769 communities; 2019: 3, 2020: 4, 2022: 702, 2026: 60). Shown as published; conditions may have changed since the visit.
  • Fuel prices are compared with the median of communities surveyed in the same year, because prices rose sharply between 2022 and 2026.
  • Nearest MV line, substation and mini-grid are recalculated against the current layers and the combined mini-grid list.
  • The grey tick on each generator bar marks the survey-wide median.
