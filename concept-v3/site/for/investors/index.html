A · Field-verifiedMore than 5 km from the grid, not served, and a NESP field survey within 2 km.
B · Large off-gridMore than 5 km from the grid, not served, 1,000+ buildings.
C · Mid-size off-gridMore than 5 km from the grid, not served, below 1,000 buildings.
D · Near gridWithin 5 km of the MV grid: interconnected mini-grid or grid extension, with the DisCo.
ServedA recorded mini-grid already lies within the distance you choose.
Indicative figures: demo assumptions Demo only

Sizing and cost figures on this page are worked out in your browser from the assumptions below. They show the logic only; they are not REA or programme figures. Change any value and every figure updates.

connections = buildings × connections per building · daily demand = connections × demand per connection · PV size (kWp) = daily demand ÷ (yield × performance ratio) · capital cost = kWp × cost per kWp · grant = connections × grant per connection, capped at the capital cost

How this workspace is made

  • Sites: SE4ALL off-grid settlements with distances recalculated against the field-mapped MV grid on 24 November 2026. Mini-grids: the combined list of 312 sites from three public sources; see the mini-grid map for sources and terms.
  • "Off-grid buildings per recorded kW" compares the buildings in off-grid settlements with recorded mini-grid capacity in the state. Higher means more unmet demand per kW already built. Recorded capacity is incomplete, so treat it as a first screen.
  • For context, DARES targets 1,225 new mini-grids and $750 million of finance (REA).
  • Settlement names, population and estimated demand come from the World Bank's Distributed Renewable Energy Atlas for Nigeria (VIDA; CC BY 4.0), matched to the nearest DRE settlement within 1 km (15,041 of 17,574 sites). Where there is no match, demand comes from the demo assumptions.
  • All money and kWp figures are demo calculations from the assumptions above. They show the method; they are not REA estimates.
