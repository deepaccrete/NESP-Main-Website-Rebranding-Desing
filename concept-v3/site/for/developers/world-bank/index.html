Indicative figures: demo assumptions Demo only

Sizing and cost figures on this page are worked out in your browser from the assumptions below. They show the logic only; they are not REA or programme figures. Change any value and every figure updates.

connections = buildings × connections per building · daily demand = connections × demand per connection · PV size (kWp) = daily demand ÷ (yield × performance ratio) · capital cost = kWp × cost per kWp · grant = connections × grant per connection, capped at the capital cost

How this workspace is made

  • Sites: the 17,574 settlements in SE4ALL's off-grid settlement layer (cluster_offgrid_simple). "Off-grid" means more than 5 km from the field-mapped MV grid; "near grid" means 5 km or less (interconnection or grid-extension candidates). Distances recalculated on 24 November 2026.
  • Nearest mini-grid uses the combined list of 312 sites on the mini-grid map. Schools, health centres and markets are as recorded in the settlement layer.
  • "Field-surveyed" means a community in the NESP field survey lies within 2 km; its full record is one click away.
  • Settlement names, population and estimated demand come from the World Bank's Distributed Renewable Energy Atlas for Nigeria (VIDA; CC BY 4.0), matched to the nearest DRE settlement within 1 km (15,041 of 17,574 sites). Where there is no match, demand comes from the demo assumptions.
  • Indicative kWp and costs are demo calculations from the assumptions above, not REA figures.
