Why GIS

Skills the energy sector needs

Electrification planning runs on maps: finding communities without power, measuring their distance to the grid, estimating demand and siting mini-grids. The platform's own team learned these skills through the same courses and mapathons.

  • Read and analyse settlement, grid and survey data in QGIS.
  • Map buildings from satellite imagery on OpenStreetMap.
  • Turn data into project decisions for developers and government.
