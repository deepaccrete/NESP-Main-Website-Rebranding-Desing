How this page is made

  • Source: the project map on nep.rea.gov.ng (Rural Electrification Agency, Nigeria Electrification Project), read on 24 November 2026. The mini-grid list is labelled April 2021 at source. No licence is stated on that site; republishing is subject to REA's agreement.
  • Project records are read from the data the NEP map publishes. They are not changed here, only checked: each point is placed in a state by its coordinates, and NEP mini-grids within 1 km of a site in the SE4ALL mini-grid layer are counted once.
  • Health facility system sizes are withheld because all 100 records carry the same placeholder value. Phase II and III "grant" fields hold row numbers rather than amounts and are withheld too.
  • Solar home system figures are state totals with one point per state, not individual installations.

Records flagged for REA

  • 0020. Ajokpachi-Odo (mini-grid): listed as Kogi, plotted in Benue.

  • Cottage Hospital Dyerok (health facility): listed as Niger, plotted in Plateau.
  • General Hospital Ifo (health facility): listed as Ogun, plotted in Lagos.
  • 4 records share exact coordinates with another record of the same programme.
