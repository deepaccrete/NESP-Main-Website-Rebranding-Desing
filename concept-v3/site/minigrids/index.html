How this map is made

  • Compiled on 24 November 2026 from the SE4ALL minigrids layer, the REA mini-grid file published by WRI's Energy Access Explorer (content dated 2021) and the NEP project map (list labelled April 2021). Statuses are normalised; the original wording is kept in the download.
  • When sources disagree (name, capacity, connections), the site panel shows each value. The combined record prefers the SE4ALL layer, then REA via EAE, then NEP.
  • OpenStreetMap solar plants up to 1 MW are shown as possible mini-grids only. They are not counted, and they are downloadable separately because the ODbL licence is share-alike.
  • Status dates differ by source and some are years old. Confirm current status with REA or the developer before relying on it.
