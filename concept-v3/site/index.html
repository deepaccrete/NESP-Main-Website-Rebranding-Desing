Why this platform

What makes it different

Other sources map buildings from satellites. This platform went to the communities, mapped the grid on the ground, and turns both into answers.

769

Field-verified demand data

Teams visited each community and recorded about 100 facts: who runs generators, what fuel costs, which mills, shops and clinics are there, and whether mobile money and 3G work.

A field-mapped grid, measured against every settlement

Medium-voltage lines from field surveys, mobile mapping, DisCo data and 2025 work orders. All 454,267 settlements are re-measured against it every night; 20,550 changed side of the 5 km line.
