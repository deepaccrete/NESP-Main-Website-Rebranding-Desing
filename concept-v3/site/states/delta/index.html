    How far buildings are from the grid

    92% of Delta's 667,447 buildings are within 1 km of the mapped MV grid and 4% are more than 5 km away (Nigeria: 14%). Most buildings are close to the grid, so supply quality and connections matter more than new lines; off-grid options suit the remote fringe.

    About these figures and the data behind them
    • Calculated on 23 November 2026 from the Nigeria SE4ALL public data portal (data.nigeriase4all.gov.ng), run by the Rural Electrification Agency. The map's lines and settlements, and the click-for-details lookup, are read live from that portal's GeoServer.
    • Distance to grid is recalculated for every settlement against the current field-mapped MV layer (about 50 m accuracy). Line lengths are clipped to the state boundary.
    • "No working grid" means a surveyed community with no grid, grid that has never worked, or grid present but not connected. Survey years run from 2019 to 2026.
    • Mini-grids: the platform layer plus NEP mini-grids (REA project map, list labelled April 2021) that are not already in it. Substations and plants are de-duplicated by name.
    • Layers: cluster_all_simple_new, cluster_offgrid_simple, cluster_offgrid_survey, distribution_line_mv, existing_lines_high_voltage, substations_currently_installed, minigrids, disco_coverage. Dataset licences are not yet specified on the portal.
