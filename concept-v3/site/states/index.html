How these figures are made

  • Settlement and building figures: SE4ALL settlement and off-grid layers, summed by state. Distances are recalculated for every settlement against the field-mapped MV grid (distribution_line_mv).
  • MV grid: distribution_line_mv, clipped to state boundaries. Clinics, grid condition and build-ready sites: field-survey layer.
  • Calculated from the public data portal on 23 November 2026; planned refresh: nightly.
