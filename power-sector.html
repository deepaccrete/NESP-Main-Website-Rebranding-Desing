Power sector

Tracking Nigeria’s power sector and the 30:30:30 targets

The electrification sector has undergone fundamental changes — in technologies (e.g. decentralised renewables), in planning actors (e.g. public-private partnerships) — and is today increasingly digital. Here we bring together data on the power sector as a whole: national progress towards the 30:30:30 objectives, and maps of the transmission network and DisCo coverage.

National electrification targets

Explore progress on the national targets

Following the SE4ALL Global Action Agenda, the Nigerian government committed to reach two targets by 2030. The underlying data comes from an extensive market survey in 2020; projections are based on official numbers for planned power assets.
