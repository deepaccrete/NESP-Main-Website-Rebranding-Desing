How these figures are made

  • Off-grid buildings: SE4ALL off-grid settlement layer, summed by state. ">5 km" uses distances recalculated against the field-mapped MV grid.
  • Mini-grids: the platform's mini-grid layer (66 sites), assigned to states by location. It does not yet include all programme mini-grids.
  • Calculated from the public data portal on 23 November 2026; planned refresh: nightly.
