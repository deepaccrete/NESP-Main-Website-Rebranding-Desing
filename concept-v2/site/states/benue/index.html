How these figures are made

  • Calculated from the public data portal on 23 November 2026; planned refresh: nightly.
  • Line lengths are clipped to the state boundary. The MV layer combines SE4ALL field data, DisCo grid data (KAECO, KEDCO) and 2025 field work orders.
  • Distance to grid is recalculated for every settlement against the current MV layer (accurate to about 50 m).
  • Survey records are shown as published (survey years 2019 to 2026). Dataset licences are not yet specified on the portal.
