Fixed automatically on this site

  • Duplicate substations, power plants and other repeated rows are removed before counting.
  • Every settlement's distance to grid is recalculated against the current field-mapped MV layer.
  • Survey communities get their nearest MV line and substation.
  • Every figure shows its source layer and the date it was pulled.

Needs the data owners

  • Remove duplicates at source and block repeat imports.
  • Add created and updated dates to every record.
  • Choose a licence for every layer.
  • Align the webmaps and the data portal on one source of truth.
  • Complete the mini-grid layer with programme data; extend the survey to the remaining states.
