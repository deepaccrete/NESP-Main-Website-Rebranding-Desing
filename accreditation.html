How to credit our data

When you use Nigeria SE4ALL data in a report, map, app or model, please credit it like this:

Nigeria SE4ALL, Rural Electrification Agency (2026). [Dataset name], version of [last update date]. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. https://nigeriase4all.gov.ng

Where a dataset includes OpenStreetMap data, also include “© OpenStreetMap contributors”.
